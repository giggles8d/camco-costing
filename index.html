How to read this & what to trust
  • Time span: use the period bar at the top (global) or each tab's Change control to set the window. Tabs default to the global window until you override them. Every figure on a tab recomputes for its window.
  • Spine = Costing_SoProfitLoss at shop-order grain (dated by close). Margin is always dollar-weighted (ΣProfit ÷ ΣRevenue); the per-SO cost already blends material + labor + overhead burden.
  • Two revenue lenses: Job P&L uses DollarValue by close date; the Revenue tab uses invoice TotalCost by ship date — they won't match exactly.
  • Material joins purchasing to jobs on the R-stripped SO# (job R23653 ↔ PO 23653); categories use [Acc Code] → Purchasing_AccountingCodes. A large share of PO spend has a blank vendor — shown explicitly.
  • Accurate Cost (WI-FIN-001): material + clocked hours (Production_TimeSheetData) × all-in rate (direct wage $21.35 + burden $79.03). Burden rate from Production_OverheadData; reconciled to the QuickBooks P&L. Burden was last set 2025-07 (stale).
  • Quote vs Actual compares Costing_Quote (AverageMargin) to realized job margin recomputed for the chosen window.
  • Refresh: re-run Refresh-Dashboard.ps1 (read-only) to pull current data, then re-open.
